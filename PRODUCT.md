## Users & purpose
Mauro Gilardi uses the admin as a private Gönner- & Finanz-Plattform: manage supporters, record payments back to 2022, see per-person last-year and lifetime totals, and process form inquiries (100er Club / Birdie / Eagle / Albatros / Sponsoring).

## Brand personality
Clean, premium, precise — Swiss PGA red (#d71920) as accent on a restrained near-black / off-white product surface.

## Anti-references
Generic purple dashboards, noisy card stacks, decorative motion, display fonts in data UI.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast for data tables and forms; keyboard-reachable actions; clear paid/open status.
